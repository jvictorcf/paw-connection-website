Why Online Dog Training Works Better Than You Think

2 Sep 2025


When most people picture dog training, they imagine a trainer arriving at their home or working directly with their dog. That can feel like the “real” way to do it. But here’s something many dog parents don’t realise: in-person training often adds extra stress and excitement that makes it harder for your dog to learn.

Many of the dogs I help are puppies, highly excitable, or sometimes stressed and reactive. For these dogs, having a new person in the house can feel overwhelming. Even the friendliest dog can get overstimulated by a visitor. Instead of making things easier, the presence of a trainer can actually make it harder to focus and practice calm behaviour.

That’s why I use virtual consultations. They aren’t just a convenient alternative — they’re often the kindest, calmest, and most effective way to support you and your dog.

No More Barriers to Accessing Support

Frustrated your trainer isn't available anymore? Moved to a new area? With virtual consultations, you don't lose your trainer — your support is as reliable as your internet connection. You get expert guidance anytime, anywhere, without travel or lost progress. More flexibility, more focus, and consistent results, all from your home.

Why Online Dog Training Works Better Than You Think

By working together online, we remove the biggest obstacle: the stress of a stranger in your dog’s space. This means:

  • Your dog stays relaxed at home in their familiar environment.

  • I get to observe real behaviour, not the “performance” that often happens when a new person is around.

  • You, the dog parent, stay fully engaged — learning, asking questions, and practicing without worrying about how your dog will react to me.

After our session, you’ll have:

  • Clear demonstrations and video examples.

  • Helpful visual aids shared during our consultation to make each step easy to follow.

  • A step-by-step written plan to guide you forward.

“But Will It Really Work for My Dog?”

This is the question I hear most often — and it’s a fair one. The short answer? Yes. Virtual consultations are designed for dogs at all stages:

  • Puppies exploring the world.

  • Energetic dogs who adore people (sometimes a little too much).

  • Sensitive or reactive dogs who find new experiences overwhelming.

The truth is, most behaviours we find challenging — like pulling on lead, barking at visitors, or jumping up — come from high arousal or stress. We don’t need to rehearse those behaviours in front of me; you already see them every day. What makes the difference is learning how to:

  • Spot your dog’s subtle calming signals.

  • Build on the moments when they’re making good choices.

  • Use everyday games and activities to make learning fun and natural.

  • Teach calmness and self-control in a way your dog can succeed.

  • Do all of this without needing any prior training experience — I’ll guide you step by step.

That’s the foundation of lasting change.
