About me

3 Jun 2025

 

Hello, I’m Luciana

I'm a Canine Nutritionist and Behaviour Consultant, and my work is about helping dogs and their people build a more harmonious relationship — one grounded in understanding what your dog actually needs: not just how they behave, but how they feel, and how what they eat shapes both.

My Journey with Thor

My journey started with Thor, my own dog. His challenges led me far beyond traditional training and sparked a deep interest in applied ethology, canine nutrition, and science-based approaches to behaviour. Supporting Thor through his fear, reactivity, and later his health needs didn't just shape my career — it inspired a lifelong mission to understand what truly helps dogs live with balance, confidence, and wellbeing.

Why This Work Matters to Me

I know how it feels to be overwhelmed, spending months unsure of what to do, surrounded by conflicting advice. That's why I'm committed to offering clear, compassionate guidance that looks at the whole dog — because nutrition and behaviour are more connected than most people realise, and real change happens when we address both.

Qualifications and Approach

I hold a BSc in Dietetics and an advanced qualification in Canine Nutrition (The Science Dog, Linda Case), alongside professional certification in Applied Ethology in Dogs — an approach rooted in the science of animal behaviour in natural and domestic environments, looking at the dog as a whole: their learning, environment, genetics, and health.

How I Can Support You

I work entirely online, helping you understand the why behind your dog's behaviour and health, so we can find realistic, sustainable solutions together — wherever you are. Not sure where to start? I offer a free discovery call so we can work out together what your dog needs most.
